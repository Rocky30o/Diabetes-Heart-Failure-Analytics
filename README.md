# Diabetes & Heart Failure Survival Analysis Dashboard (Tableau)

## Overview

This Tableau dashboard provides an in-depth analysis of patient health records to understand the impact of various medical conditions and clinical indicators on survival outcomes. The dashboard focuses on factors such as diabetes, anaemia, high blood pressure, smoking habits, age, and several laboratory measurements to identify patterns associated with patient mortality.

Using interactive visualizations, the project transforms healthcare data into meaningful insights that can assist researchers, healthcare professionals, and students in understanding risk factors related to heart failure and survival.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze the relationship between diabetes and patient survival.
- Study the impact of age, gender, and lifestyle factors on mortality.
- Evaluate how clinical measurements influence survival outcomes.
- Identify key risk factors associated with heart failure patients.
- Support data-driven healthcare analysis through visual storytelling.

## Insights Generated

- Diabetes is a significant factor affecting survival outcomes.
- Older patients generally exhibit higher mortality risk.
- Smoking and hypertension contribute to poorer survival rates.
- Clinical indicators such as serum creatinine and ejection fraction strongly influence patient outcomes.
- Gender-based survival differences can be explored through comparative analysis.
- Survival duration varies significantly across patient groups and health conditions.
